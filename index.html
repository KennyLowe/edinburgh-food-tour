THE LOVELY LITTLE DETOUR

Take the lane less hurried

Circus Lane

Circus Lane, Edinburgh

Cobbles, climbing greenery and a church tower at the end of the view. This tiny residential lane is one of the city’s loveliest slow walks.

Good to knowPeople live here: keep voices down, stay out of doorways and enjoy the details without blocking the lane.

A market-day meander

Stockbridge Market at Saunders Street is a lovely Sunday detour for street food and small producers. Swap it for lunch, not another full meal. Check the current schedule first.

A tucked-away garden beside Duddingston Loch. Save it for another morning or take a taxi: it is a separate outing, not on this walking route. Check seasonal access.

Opening times & dinner plans

This is a curated suggestion, not live availability. Check each venue’s hours, menu and access before travelling. The cocktail stop depends on opening time; go straight to dinner if needed. Reserve Gaucho Edinburgh for around 19:00 yourself. Nothing has been booked.

Gaucho Edinburgh — venue & reservations ↗
Budget & appetite

Allow roughly £100–£160 per person for the full day, depending on steak, sides and drinks. This is a planning estimate, not quoted menu prices. Share the cheese snack and keep lunch light. Water and alcohol-free drinks work just as well.

Weather, daylight & access

Bring a waterproof and comfortable shoes. Dean Village and the riverside have slopes, uneven paving and potentially slippery paths. In wet weather, use streets instead of the Water of Leith path. For step-free needs, check your route and each venue directly. Outdoor stops are early to suit shorter days.

A rainy-day version

Shorten Dean Village, linger over lunch and spend more time in the Portrait Gallery. Check exhibitions and opening hours; special exhibitions may charge. A taxi between Stockbridge and New Town is an easy alternative to the uphill walk.
